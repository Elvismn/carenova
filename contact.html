Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve healthcare institutions across Kenya, with extended services throughout East Africa including Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, and Ethiopia. For international inquiries, please contact us for specific arrangements.

What is your typical response time?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 business hours. For emergency maintenance requests, we respond within 2 hours and can dispatch technicians within 4 hours for critical equipment in Nairobi.

Do you offer installation services?

Yes, we provide complete installation and commissioning services for all equipment we supply. This includes site assessment, installation, calibration, testing, and staff training.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, cheques, and major credit cards. For large institutional orders, we can arrange flexible payment plans and credit terms based on established relationships.

Do you provide training for equipment use?

Yes, comprehensive training is included with all equipment purchases. We provide both initial training during installation and ongoing training as needed.

How do I request service for equipment not purchased from you?

We provide maintenance services for most brands and models of biomedical equipment, regardless of where it was purchased. Contact our technical support team with your equipment details for an assessment.
