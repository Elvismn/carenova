About Carenova Biotech

Your trusted partner in healthcare innovation and excellence

Who We Are

Carenova Biotech Limited is a leading medical supplies company headquartered at Topmark House, Buruburu, Nairobi, Kenya. Guided by our tagline "Innovating Care. Delivering Trust.", we specialize in providing comprehensive healthcare solutions to institutions across East Africa.

Our commitment goes beyond supplying products – we build long-term partnerships with healthcare providers by combining technical expertise, professional integrity, and customer-focused solutions.
